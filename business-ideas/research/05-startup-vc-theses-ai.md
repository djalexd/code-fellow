## 2. Traction vs. saturation
**Traction:** Lovable ~$600M ARR (Aug 2026) ([AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/news/lovable-europes-vibe-coding-platform-crosses-us-600m-arr)); Legora $100M in 18 months ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/legora-just-hit-100-million-in-revenue-it-took-18-months)); Langdock ~€50M ARR ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/langdock-hits-50m-arr-55m)); **Minerva (Poland, AI for public tenders) >$2.5M ARR, clients won 1,300 tenders worth PLN 1.25B** ([WBJ](https://wbj.pl/ai-targets-tenders/post/149513)); solo founders at $1M+ ARR in narrow niches ([arr.club](https://www.arr.club/signal/reddgrow-crossed-1m-arr-8-months-bootstrapped-solo)).

**Saturated / crowded:** AI SDRs (11x reported 70–80% churn) ([aisdr](https://aisdr.com/blog/ai-sdr-churn.md)); vibe coding; horizontal legal AI; medical/dental voice receptionists (Vocca $20M, CiaoDott, Linda, Dentio; EU voice AI $536M in H1 2026) ([HIT Consultant](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/10/05/vocca-raises-20m-series-a-norrsken-vc-ai-phone-assistant-medical-practices/)); freight-forwarding agents (5U AI, Nexcade $6M, Conlio €6.4M); DACH/UK property management (Theo €25M, Lette $6.5M); DE/DK/NL AI accounting (Sumary $25M seed, Integral €18M, LimeTax €6M) ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/11/10/sumary-secures-25m-to-expand-its-accounting-software-across-europe/)).

**Gap:** funded vertical agents target DACH/UK/FR/IT/Nordics. **RO, BG, HU, SK SMB markets are absent.** Local-language documents and national systems (SICAP, e-Factura, SAF-T) are a moat.

## 3. Candidates (ranked)

### #1 AI bid desk for CEE public tenders (RO → BG/HU/SK → cross-border TED)
- Comparables: Minerva (PL) >$2.5M ARR; Cato (IT) €6M ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/09/07/cato-raises-eur6m-to-expand-its-ai-platform-for-public-sector-sales/)); Altura €8M ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/06/09/altura-raises-eur8m-for-ai-powered-bid-management-platform/)); Forgent €4.3M ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/09/23/forgent-ai-secures-eur43m-to-transform-public-procurement/)).
- ICP: Romanian SMEs bidding regularly (construction, IT, medical supplies, facilities; 10–250 staff) + bid consultancies.
- MVP (4–6 wks): ingest SICAP + TED → fit scoring → 200-page *caiet de sarcini* → requirements matrix + DUAE/ESPD checklist → draft technical proposal from past bids → clarifications watch.
- Pricing €150–500/mo + optional done-for-you bids €800–2,500 **(est.)**. GTM: award notices = free prospect list; Romanian outbound; SEO per tender.
- Competitors: licitatie-publica.ro (since 2007, AI summaries), sicap.ai, DataDriven.ro ([Apify](https://apify.com/apnoda/seap-sicap-tenders.md)) — monitoring only; differentiate on drafting + compliance.
- €1M ARR ≈ 280 customers × ~€300/mo **(est.)**. Funders: OpenOcean (Minerva), Keen (Cato), Nucleo €34M RO/CEE fund ([The Recursive](https://www.therecursive.com/cee-startup-tech-weekly-nucleo-ventures-launches-eur34m-fund-to-back-startups-across-romania-and-cee.md)), Credo €74M pre-seed fund ([Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/venture-capital/news/credo-ventures-launches-over-74m-new-fund-to-back-cee-startups-at-pre-seed-4f40485e-3640-4eff-ae7f-35796593db6c)).
- Risks: Minerva/Mimira expanding south; bid-error liability; SICAP data quality; RO market smaller than PL.

### #2 Autopilot back office for CEE accounting firms (option: AI-native firm / roll-up)
- ~33k CECCAR members ([Accountancy Europe](https://accountancyeurope.eu/fact-figure/romania/)); e-Factura + SAF-T (D406) + D394 duplicate reporting; CECCAR protested ([StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/contabili-ceccar-etva-extindere-efactura-presiune-firme-birocratie-4-propuneri-ministerul-27527)).
- MVP (6–8 wks): pull e-Factura XML + bank statements → auto-post → export to Saga/WinMentor/SmartBill → missing-document chaser (email/WhatsApp) → SAF-T pre-checks. €4–10/client/month **(est.)**.
- €1M ARR ≈ 60 firms × ~€1.4k/mo **(est.)**. Risks: incumbents add AI; price sensitivity; slow adoption.

### #3 EU-funds & grants autopilot for CEE SMEs/municipalities
- Granter €1.6M targets ES/DE/IT ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/11/03/portuguese-ai-startup-granter-bags-eur16m-to-expand-across-europe/)); Romania ~61% PNRR absorption ([StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/rezultate-pnrr-rata-preliminara-de-absorbtie-a-granturilor-si-a-imprumuturilor-pentru-romania-2021-2026-106738)). Timing risk: RRF ended Aug 2026; depends on 2021–27 cohesion funds. Best as a module of #1.
