## 2. Ranked candidates

### #1 CRA Desk — vulnerability reporting + CRA technical file for SME hardware/firmware/software makers
- **Deadline:** reporting live since 11 Sep 2026; full conformity 11 Dec 2027. Fine cap €15M / 2.5% turnover (Art. 64 — from model knowledge; verify).
- **ICP:** EU makers of connected devices, industrial controllers, embedded systems (10–500 staff); B2B software vendors into the EU. Tens of thousands obligated **(est.)**.
- **Wedge (4–8 wks):** SBOM ingestion (CI/firmware) → CVE/VEX monitoring → "actively exploited?" alerts (CISA KEV + EPSS) → one-click 24h/72h/final report drafts in ENISA SRP format + on-call playbook + audit trail. Later: Annex VII technical file + DoC generator; Data Act module.
- **Pricing:** €290–990 per product line/month; ACV ~€6–15k **(est.)**.
- **GTM:** test labs / future notified bodies; embedded consultancies; long-tail SEO ("CRA Article 14 report template"); EU SECURE call — **€11.5M cascade funding for SME CRA work** ([cyberresilienceact.eu](https://www.cyberresilienceact.eu/de/news/secure-second-open-call-1-october-2026-cra-sme-funding.html)).
- **Competitors:** CRACI €1.4M pre-seed, waitlist-only ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/05/19/craci-raises-eur14m-for-eu-cybersecurity-compliance-platform)); Verimu €49/mo, CRA-Ready $49–99/mo stop at SBOM ([Verimu](https://verimu.com/pricing), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10045966/CRA-Ready/)); ONEKEY / Cybellum enterprise-priced.
- **€1M ARR:** ~110 customers × €9k **(est.)**.
- **Risks:** Omnibus single entry point (content unchanged), late harmonised standards, funded entrants.

### #2 Pay-transparency "first report" kit — 150–1,000 staff employers, CEE-first
- First reports 7 Jun 2027; ≥5% unexplained gap triggers joint pay assessment ([Trusaic](https://trusaic.com/regulatory-pay-transparency-reporting/eu/)). 60–80k EU employers with 150+ staff **(est.)**.
- Wedge: payroll CSV → AI job evaluation / equal-work grouping → Art. 9 metrics → gap narrative → employee request portal. €3–12k/yr.
- Competitors: Payslip Lecora, SkillsTrust, Ravio ($12M A), Figures, Trusaic — Western Europe/English focus ([GPA](https://gpa.net/blogs/emea-1/emea-payslip-launches-eu-pay-transparency-reporting-platform), [tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/05/09/ravio-secures-12m-to-improve-the-quality-of-compensation-data/)).
- Risks: transposition lag; post-report churn.

### #3 E-invoicing "accountant cockpit" for CEE micro/SMB clients (bootstrapped play)
- 2027 cluster: PL micro Jan 2027, SK/HR Jan 2027, DE issuing Jan 2027, FR SMEs Sep 2027.
- Multi-client inbox across KSeF / e-Factura / Peppol, AI EN 16931 repair, rejected-invoice fixer, export to local accounting software. €2–5/client/month.
- Competitors: DDD Invoices (€1.31M), A-Cube (€4M), Avalara, Sovos, ERPs ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/05/14/ddd-invoices-raises-eur131m-to-simplify-global-e-invoicing-compliance/)).

### #4 EAA remediation for documents/PDFs (lower priority)
Overlay/scanner space funded (DevAlly €2M, QualiBooth €2M, Nexus Inclusion €3.5M); accessible PDFs in CEE languages less served. Enforcement weak.

## 3. Recommendation
**#1 CRA Desk** has the best fit: live, not delayed, SMB tail ignored by incumbents, EU money subsidises buyers, hard Dec 2027 date. **#2** is the best second bet. Avoid CSRD/CSDDD, CBAM tail, EUDR, AI Act high-risk tooling.
