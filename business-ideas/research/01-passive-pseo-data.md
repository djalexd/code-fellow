# Passive Scout #1 — Content, data & programmatic-SEO assets

*Role: Passive-Income Scout #1. Keyword volumes are Semrush estimates pulled 16 Nov 2026. **(est.)** = scout's own estimate.*

## 1. What practitioners report (2025–2026)
- **Content sites lost value:** Empire Flippers multiples ~27x (2024) → ~24x (2025) ([EF](https://empireflippers.com/you-can-sell-your-business-for-life-changing-money-in-2025-heres-the-secret/)); Flippa content-site values reportedly −33.5% in 2025 ([summary](https://click-science-ventures.pages.dev/insights/how-small-internet-businesses-change-hands/)).
- **Big brands + UGC dominate SERPs:** Glen Allsopp, 250k SERPs, no independent site among top content domains ([Detailed](https://detailed.com/state-of-content/), [Detailed](https://detailed.com/google-control/)).
- **Indie money went to software:** Photo AI ~$100–155k/mo ([IH](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/photo-ai-by-pieter-levels-complete-deep-dive-case-study-0-to-132k-mrr-in-18-months-3a9a2b1579)); Nomad List $20–40k/mo at peak ([levels.io](https://levels.io/nomad-list-founder)).
- **Directories pay modestly:** OpenAlternative ~$6.5k MRR, 35% from $197/mo featured listings ([Dirstarter](https://dirstarter.com/blog/openalternative-case-study)); niche directories $500–5k/mo year one ([Dirstarter](https://dirstarter.com/blog/how-much-do-directory-websites-make)).
- **Tools/calculators beat articles** ([Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/?p=141177)); Kevin Indig: SEO "decoupled" from traffic ([Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/the-great-decoupling)).

## 2. Reality check
- **AI Overviews:** top-result CTR −34.5% (Apr 2025) → −58% (Dec 2025) per Ahrefs; Seer −61% informational ([SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-drive-drop-organic-paid-ctr-464212), [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/googles-ai-summaries-now-swallow-58-of-clicks-that-once-went-to-websites/)); Pew 8% vs 15% click rate ([SEW](https://www.searchengineworld.com/pew-data-confirms-50-reduction-in-serp-click-through-rates)).
- **Where AIO appears:** 21.4% informational vs 4.3% commercial, 2.1% transactional, 0.9% navigational ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-triggers/)); local 7.9% vs 22.8% ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overviews-appear-on-21-of-searches-new-data/560471/)). → **Event/date/booking pages are relatively safe; "how to" is not.**
- **Scaled content abuse** applies however pages are made; spam updates Mar/Jun/Aug/Sep 2026 ([ppc.land](https://ppc.land/scaled-content-abuse/), [GSQI](https://gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/)); Google May 2026 "non-commodity" guidance ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/05/a-new-resource-for-optimizing)).
- **HouseFresh** −91%, ~2 years to recover; no brand searches ([SEL](https://searchengineland.com/review-site-google-traffic-affiliate-seo-content-440143)).
- **GEO:** LLMs cite Reddit/Wikipedia/news; adding statistics raises visibility ([Digital Bloom](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/2025-ai-citation-llm-visibility-report/)). Original numbers get cited.
- **Ads:** Raptive min 25k PV/mo; Mediavine Journey ~1k sessions ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/)).

## 4. Candidates
- **A. Multilingual race-calendar pages (DE/FR/ES/IT/NL) from EuroSlam data** — month × distance × country pages with weather history, elevation, trip-cost index. Build 7–10 days; 2–3h/wk; month 12 €400–1,500/mo **(est.)**. Risk: thin translations = scaled content abuse.
- **B. Automated weekly "Race Radar" newsletter** — registrations, ballots, price rises, sell-outs. Sponsors $20–50 per 1k subs; <5k subs $50–250/placement ([beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/newsletter-sponsorship-cost)). Build 3–5 days; 1–2h/wk; €150–700/mo **(est.)**.
- **C. Hyrox calendar + "what's a good time" percentile calculator** — ~1.5M athletes 2025/26 ([BoxRox](https://www.boxrox.com/?p=248004)). Build 5–7 days; 2h/wk; €300–2,000/mo **(est.)**. Risk: competitors ([hyranking](https://hyranking.com/learn/insights/are-hyrox-athletes-actually-getting-faster)), brand/terms.
- **D. European Race PB & Weather Index** — annual report + CSV/API; median Gumroad seller $72/mo ([InsightRaider](https://insightraider.com/en/blog/best-sources-gumroad-statistics)), so mainly a link/citation magnet. €100–800/mo **(est.)**.
- **E. Featured listings for organisers** — €29–99/mo; needs sales; month 9+.
